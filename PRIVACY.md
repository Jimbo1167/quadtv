# Privacy Policy for QuadTV

**Last Updated:** October 8, 2025
**Effective Date:** October 8, 2025

## Overview

QuadTV is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information or browsing data.

## Data Collection

**QuadTV does NOT collect:**
- Personal information
- Browsing history
- User credentials
- Analytics or usage statistics
- Any data that leaves your browser

## Local Storage Only

QuadTV stores the following data **locally on your device only** using browser localStorage:

1. **Layout Preferences** - Your last selected layout (2x2, 1+2, or 2-vertical)
2. **Grid Sizing Ratios** - Custom stream sizes when you drag dividers to resize

This data:
- ✅ Stays on your local device
- ✅ Never leaves your browser
- ✅ Is not transmitted to any server
- ✅ Is not shared with any third party
- ✅ Can be cleared by clearing browser data or clicking "Reset Grid Sizing"

## Permissions Usage

QuadTV requests minimal permissions to function:

- **activeTab**: Access the current YouTube TV tab to inject the multi-view grid
- **tabs**: Query the active tab to ensure QuadTV only runs on YouTube TV pages
- **storage**: Save layout preferences and grid sizing locally in your browser
- **tv.youtube.com**: Restrict extension to only run on YouTube TV pages

See [PERMISSIONS.md](PERMISSIONS.md) for detailed justification of each permission.

## Third-Party Services

QuadTV does **not** use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

## YouTube TV Content

QuadTV displays YouTube TV content in multiple iframes within a single browser tab. All content is loaded directly from `tv.youtube.com` - QuadTV does not intercept, modify, or store any video content or user data from YouTube TV.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date and notify users through the extension update notes.
