Product Brief: QuadTV for Firefox
Mission Statement (Our North Star)

To provide the most fluid, intuitive, and powerful multi-stream viewing experience for YouTube TV users, transforming a passive viewing session into an active, command-center experience.
The Problem

Watching multiple live events on YouTube TV's desktop web interface is impossible without clumsy, manual window management. The native "multiview" feature is limited to specific devices and pre-selected, rigid combinations, stripping power users of choice and flexibility. Sports fans can't build their perfect Sunday dashboard, and news junkies can't monitor multiple breaking stories.
The Solution

QuadTV is a Firefox browser extension that seamlessly integrates with the YouTube TV website. It allows users to instantly create flexible, customizable multi-view layouts (from two to four streams) directly within a single browser tab. With intuitive, on-screen controls for audio and stream management, QuadTV empowers users to take full command of their viewing experience.
Guiding Principles

These are the core values we will refer to when making any design or engineering decision.

    Control without Clutter: The user should feel powerful, not overwhelmed. Controls are visible when needed and disappear when not, keeping the focus on the content.

    Fluid & Fast: The experience must feel native and performant. Transitions will be smooth, and actions will be instantaneous, with no jarring page reloads.

    Intuitive by Default, Powerful on Demand: A new user should understand the core functionality in seconds. An advanced user should discover deeper features (like saved presets) that enhance their experience over time.

    Extensible by Design: The architecture will be modular, allowing us to easily add new layouts, features, or even support other streaming platforms in the future.
